# Camera frames in YUV422

**Status:** Done

Implements the colorspace decided in [camera.md](../specs/camera.md) ("Colorspace: YUV422 on the wire, BGR in the frame") and the seam rows of [robot.md](../specs/robot.md): the real robot subscribes its cameras in YUV422, the camera's own colorspace, and converts each frame to BGR on the host, so `CameraRead` and `CameraFrame` keep their BGR bytes and nothing above the seam changes. It deliberately leaves out compressing frames on the robot (camera.md open question 8) and the viewer, which has its own repository.

## Scope

- `src/naoqi_bridge/robot.py` — `CAMERA_COLORSPACE_YUV422` (9) replaces `CAMERA_COLORSPACE_BGR` (13)
- `src/naoqi_bridge/real_robot.py` — `subscribe_camera` asks for YUV422; `read_camera` converts with `yuv422_to_bgr` (BT.601, limited range, numpy imported lazily)
- `tests/test_real_robot.py` — the stub image in YUV422; the subscription's colorspace; the conversion's known colours, round trip and size check
- `tests-e2e/test_live_streams.py` — the feed's colours against Naoqi's own BGR of the top camera
- `specs/camera.md`, `specs/robot.md`, `specs/_index.md`, `specs/_overview.md`, `docs/reference/api.md` — the colorspace, the seam rows, the summaries

## Steps

1. Seam constant: `CAMERA_COLORSPACE_YUV422 = 9` in `robot.py`, exported in place of the BGR one; `CameraRead.data` stays BGR.
2. `yuv422_to_bgr(data, width, height)` in `real_robot.py`: the buffer as `(height, width / 2, 4)` pairs (`Y0 U Y1 V`), each pixel's luma and its pair's colour, BT.601 limited range in float32, rounded and clipped; a buffer that is not `width × height × 2` bytes raises `ValueError`, which the feed logs and retries as any failed read.
3. `RealNaoqiRobot.subscribe_camera` passes 9; `read_camera` converts the buffer. `SimNaoqiRobot` inherits both.
4. Fast tests on the stub `qi`: the subscription asks for 9; a read of a black-and-white pair gives BGR black then white; the conversion's known colours, round trip and refusal.
5. Live test: a BGR subscription of the test's own beside the feed, compared on the same capture (within a level on average) or on different captures (channel means within six levels).
6. Specs and docs: the colorspace section, the seam rows, the summaries.
